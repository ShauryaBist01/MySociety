Built For Modern Communities

Everything Your Society Needs, Fully Digitized

Engineered specifically to solve the core challenges of housing societies and residential complexes.

1. Add & Manage Society

Presidents can create their society profile, set total flat capacity, address, and manage the central society administration digitally.

2. Dynamic Maintenance Calculator

Automated calculation logic combining fixed base charges (₹500) plus exact monthly fees for only the facilities opted by each flat.

3. Facility Complaint Ticketing

Residents easily log complaints linked directly to facilities. Presidents review, update statuses (Pending, In Progress, Resolved), and reply.

4. Facility-to-Flat Availment

Total transparency on which amenity (Gym, Pool, Parking, Clubhouse) has been availed by which flat, complete with resident names and dates.

5. Flat Occupancy Tracking

Live real-time visibility into which flats are Owned, which are Rented, and which are Empty/Unsold across the society.

6. President & Member Dashboards

President gets high-level analytics, member management, and billing tools; residents enjoy self-service amenity selection and bill viewing.
